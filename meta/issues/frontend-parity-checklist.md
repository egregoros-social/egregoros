# Work through the frontend parity checklist

## Summary

`docs/frontend-checklist.md` tracks web UI parity against Mastodon/Pleroma
clients, with items marked DONE/PARTIAL/TODO and ordered roughly by what
matters for a daily driver. The PARTIAL and TODO items are the remaining UI
backlog.

## Requirements

- Work the P0 items before P1 and below.
- Update the status column in the checklist as items land.
- Split any item large enough to need its own design discussion into its own
  issue rather than growing this one.

## Acceptance Criteria

- All P0 items are DONE or have their own issue explaining why they are not.
- The checklist's statuses match the shipped UI.

## Notes

- This issue is a pointer to the checklist, deliberately not a copy of it.
